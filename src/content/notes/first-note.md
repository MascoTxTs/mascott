# Questions worth exploring

This is the first note in my personal archive.

I want this website to become a long-term record of what I learn, what I build, and what questions I find interesting.

At the moment, I am exploring computer science, mathematics, psychology, and brain-computer interfaces.

The goal is not to collect everything.

It is to build connections between things that seem unrelated at first.
